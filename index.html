Free, and private

Roam Across the World is free to play. It has no accounts, adverts, analytics or trackers, and the game itself sets no cookies. Your campaign, settings and tour records are kept in your browser's local storage on your device, and the game does not send them to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes that saved progress.

To load what it needs, the game asks some outside services: map tiles, Wikipedia for photo locations, Open-Meteo for the weather, and a route teaser service that passes requests to an AI model. Those requests are made like any website's, so the services receive the information the request contains, such as the place you are looking at.
